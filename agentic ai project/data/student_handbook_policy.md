# APEX INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT & TECHNOLOGY
## Official Student Policy Handbook & Academic Regulations (Batch 2025-27)

### SECTION 1: ACADEMIC CALENDAR & COURSE ENROLLMENT
#### Chunk ID: POL-ACAD-01
The academic year is organized into three trimesters. Students must register for core courses and electives during the formal registration window announced by the Registrar. Minimum attendance requirement across all enrolled courses is 75%. Students falling below 75% attendance in any course will not be permitted to sit for the end-term examination and will be awarded an 'I' (Incomplete) grade until remedial coursework is completed.

#### Chunk ID: POL-ACAD-02 (Course Change & Elective Drop/Add Policy)
Students may request a change of elective course or specialization track strictly within the first 14 calendar days (two weeks) of the trimester commencement. Course change requests are considered sensitive academic modifications and require:
1. Formal request submitted via the student portal.
2. Verification that the target course has available seat capacity.
3. Explicit approval from the Academic Programme Chair and Assigned Faculty Advisor.
No course changes are allowed after the two-week add/drop deadline under any circumstances.

### SECTION 2: TUITION FEES, PAYMENT PORTAL & REFUND REGULATIONS
#### Chunk ID: POL-FEE-01 (Fee Schedule & Deadlines)
Tuition fees for the PGDM program are payable in three installments corresponding to each academic term:
- Term 1: Payable on or before August 10th
- Term 2: Payable on or before December 15th
- Term 3: Payable on or before April 10th
Payments must be completed through the official Institute Student ERP Payment Portal via Net Banking, UPI, or Debit/Credit Card.

#### Chunk ID: POL-FEE-02 (Payment Portal Failure & Grace Period Policy)
In the event of an institutional payment portal outage, gateway timeout, or bank server failure occurring on or immediately preceding an official due date, the following provisions apply:
1. Students are granted an automatic grace period of two (2) working days (next 2 business days) to complete their fee payment without any late fine or penalty.
2. The student must retain a screenshot or reference number of the failed transaction or report the outage to Accounts Support.
3. The Finance Office will verify the gateway log and waive any system-generated late fee.

#### Chunk ID: POL-FEE-03 (Late Payment Penalties)
If fee payment is delayed beyond the published deadline (and beyond the 2-day portal failure grace period):
- A late fine of ₹500 per calendar week will be assessed for up to two weeks of delay.
- If dues remain unpaid after 15 calendar days from the deadline, access to the Learning Management System (LMS) and library resources will be temporarily suspended until cleared.

### SECTION 3: SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
#### Chunk ID: POL-SCH-01 (Merit Scholarship Eligibility & Renewal)
The Institute awards Merit Scholarships covering 25% to 50% of the tuition fee to students demonstrating exceptional academic performance:
1. Category A (50% Tuition Waiver): Awarded to students securing CGPA >= 9.0 in the preceding term with zero disciplinary infractions.
2. Category B (25% Tuition Waiver): Awarded to students securing CGPA between 8.50 and 8.99.
3. Mandatory Conditions: Recipients must maintain at least 80% attendance in all courses and actively participate in academic and institutional seminars.
4. Renewal: Scholarships are reviewed at the conclusion of each academic term based on updated CGPA.

#### Chunk ID: POL-SCH-02 (Scholarship Disbursement & Bank Details)
Scholarship amounts are disbursed directly into the student's verified bank account via direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) within 15 working days following term results declaration:
1. Students must provide their own bank account (joint accounts with parents are permitted; third-party accounts are strictly forbidden).
2. Any change to scholarship bank account details requires two-factor identity verification and explicit administrative approval from the Finance Officer to prevent fraud.

### SECTION 4: STUDENT SUPPORT, GRIEVANCES & TICKETING
#### Chunk ID: POL-SUP-01 (Support Desk & Service Level Agreements)
The Student Helpdesk provides centralized resolution for academic, technical, hostel, and administrative queries:
1. IT & ERP Portal Issues: Standard turnaround time (SLA) is 24 to 48 hours.
2. Fee & Accounts Inquiries: Standard SLA is 2 to 3 business days.
3. Hostel & Campus Amenities: Standard SLA is 24 hours for urgent maintenance, 3 days for general requests.
4. Formal Academic Appeals: Reviewed by the Academic Grievance Cell within 5 business days.

#### Chunk ID: POL-SUP-02 (Confidentiality & Privacy Rules)
Student educational records, fee statements, and scholarship details are private and confidential under Institutional Data Protection Regulations. Staff and AI automated systems are strictly prohibited from sharing any student's private record with another student. Support requests involving credential resets or sensitive updates must be initiated exclusively by the verified account holder.
